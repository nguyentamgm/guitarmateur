# Guide

Guitarmateur walks you through a three-step practice loop:

1. **[Scales & the fretboard](scales-and-fretboard.md)** — pick a key, scale, and position(s) and
   see them on the fretboard.
2. **[Progressions & licks](progressions-and-licks.md)** — build a chord progression, then get a
   practice lick for every chord, graded by difficulty.
3. **[Playback](playback.md)** — hear a metronome and your licks play back so you can practice
   along without a separate backing track.

Beyond the core loop:

- **[Tunings & left-handed view](tunings-and-view.md)** — switch tunings and mirror the fretboard
  for left-handed play.
- **[Sharing & your data](sharing-and-data.md)** — share a setup via link, export/import your data,
  and use the app offline.

Everything runs in your browser. There's no account and no server — your setup is saved to
`localStorage` on your device.
